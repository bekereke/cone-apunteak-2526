# 2.2.2 Crimpring

To produce a cable with **RJ45 connector**, we should use a tool that we will crimmp the connector. This network tool is **RJ-45 Crimping tool** and it is widely used by **network technicians**.

{% hint style="success" %}
**How to Make an Ethernet Cable?**

To make an ethernet cable, firstly cut the cable that is enough long for your requirement. Then cut the plastic sheat almost 1 inch and then order the wires according to the colors below. After that cut the wires a little again. Now, you can put the wires to the little holes of **RJ45 network connector**. Lastly, you should use **RJ45 crimping tool** to crimp the cable end. For the other end, you will also do the same steps. At the end, you can test your cable with [**Ethernet cable tester**](../2.4-cable-testing.md)**.**
{% endhint %}
